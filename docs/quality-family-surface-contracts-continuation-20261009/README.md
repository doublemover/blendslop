## Reference derivation and declared allowance

Sphere and ellipsoid facets are mapped to the authored unit sphere. The closest point to the origin on the filled triangle includes interior projections and clamped edge points. Maximum radius occurs at a vertex. Multiplying the maximum radial deviation by the largest authored radius bounds continuous world-coordinate facet deviation. The certificate includes construction rounding; checking only exact surface vertices would miss every triangle's interior error. Closed, connected, oriented sphere-topology sources, single-cycle vertex links, nondegenerate faces and outward radial orientation are required.

Analytic ellipsoid gradients at barycentric positions are positive combinations of vertex gradients. The largest vertex-to-face angle bounds that continuous normal cone. Its correspondence is radial, rather than nearest-point on an anisotropic ellipsoid; that distinction is retained explicitly. The normal policy is an engineering threshold, not a proof about all nearest-analytic-normal correspondences.

Cylinder/frustum sides use their exact linear axial radius and at most two azimuth rays per triangle. Positive radial weights preserve the short azimuth interval, so the normalized triangle's minimum radius bounds side chord error. Flat caps remain separate, correctly oriented authored planes. Capsule hemispheres use the spherical calculation about their fixed cap centers; its straight segment uses the cylindrical calculation. Unexpected joins, winding, parameter drift or overly broad azimuth intervals cannot produce a certificate.

The reconstruction allowance was declared as half a source pixel in world coordinates plus one degree of additional normal error. Actual orthographic resolution and pixel aspect determine pixel pitch, using the largest source pitch across all five views. This is an explicit engineering choice, not a measured artist preference. Candidate metrics are not inputs to the certificate or allowance. Earlier candidate results had already been visible to the humans/agents; no historical blind claim is made.
